How Simplified Lesk works

04 / METHOD
01

Retrieve candidate senses and their definitions and lexical relations.

02

Tokenize the sentence and remove common stop words and the target word.

03

Build a signature from each sense's gloss, examples and lexical relations.

04

Count distinct shared tokens; select the highest-scoring sense.

When all scores are zero, the first listed sense is selected as a flagged fallback. The curated offline senses are educational examples, not a complete or verbatim WordNet dump. Full WordNet lookup is available only with an optional NLTK installation and downloaded corpus.
